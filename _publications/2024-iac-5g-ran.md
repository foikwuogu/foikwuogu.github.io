This review examines Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) methodologies applied to the automated deployment and orchestration of 5G Radio Access Networks (RAN), Core Networks, and Service-Based Interfaces, surveying current tooling and deployment patterns relevant to next-generation carrier infrastructure.
