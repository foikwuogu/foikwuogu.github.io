## Educational Training

The five published training resources featured in the [SchoolGradeLink About section](https://schoolgradelink.com/about):

1. [AI Junior Track: A 24-Hour Beginner Training Module and Slide Deck for Early-Career Engineers (IEEE Computer Society Region 8 AI Caravan 2026)](https://zenodo.org/records/23005192) — A beginner-friendly, 24-hour AI course with an eight-session slide deck and facilitator materials.
2. [AI Viber Track: Course Materials for AI-Assisted Software Engineering (IEEE CS Region 8 AI Caravan 2026)](https://zenodo.org/records/23004667) — A 32-hour, 16-session course on AI-assisted software engineering for developers.
3. [Engineering Roles in Critical-Infrastructure Awareness: Foundations, Responsibilities, and Professional Practice for Emerging Engineer](https://zenodo.org/records/22864273) — A facilitator-led awareness training on infrastructure interdependencies, cascading failures, and professional responsibility.
4. [Engineering Roles in Infrastructure Resilience: A Mentorship Workshop for Students, Early-Career Engineers, and Professionals](https://zenodo.org/records/22858920) — A mentorship workshop package on engineering roles in infrastructure resilience.
5. [CITP-Aligned Mentorship Workshop Materials for OT/ICS & Critical Infrastructure Cybersecurity Mentees](https://zenodo.org/records/22858230) — Mentorship materials for OT/ICS and critical-infrastructure cybersecurity, aligned with the BCS Chartered IT Professional framework.
