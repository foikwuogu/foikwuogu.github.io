My software interests center on systems that combine application development, intelligent monitoring, and cyber resilience for complex operational environments. I am particularly interested in software that improves visibility, automation, decision support, and security assurance across enterprise and industrial infrastructure.

## Current Software and System Development Areas

* Application development for data-driven operational workflows and decision support
* Monitoring platforms for critical infrastructure performance, security, and resilience
* Operational technology visibility tools for oil and gas environments
* Telecom network observability and service assurance platforms
* Grid and power infrastructure monitoring, analytics, and alerting systems
* Identity, access, and governance tooling for enterprise and industrial environments
* AI-assisted anomaly detection and predictive maintenance systems
* Cloud-native dashboards, reporting systems, and orchestration utilities

Systems that assist with reliability analysis, event monitoring, risk assessment, and secure infrastructure modernization.
