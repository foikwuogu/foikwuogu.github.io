My research sits at the intersection of computer networks, software-defined infrastructure, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, and resilient digital systems. I study how intelligent control, secure architecture, and data-driven observability can be applied to communication networks and critical infrastructure environments where performance, trust, and operational continuity are essential.

I am especially interested in environments where information technology and operational technology converge, including telecommunications, energy systems, industrial control systems, cloud-native platforms, and enterprise identity ecosystems. Across these domains, my work focuses on building systems that are measurable, adaptive, secure, and practical to deploy at scale.

## Current Research Themes

* AI for network performance optimization and predictive monitoring
* Software-defined networking and secure control-plane design
* AI-assisted 5G/6G architecture, orchestration, and observability
* Zero trust architecture for enterprise and critical infrastructure systems
* Identity, access, and trust management for distributed environments
* Cloud-native and Infrastructure-as-Code approaches for network automation
* Cyber resilience for operational technology and industrial systems
* Post-quantum security for critical digital infrastructure

## Selected Research Contributions

**AI-Enabled Monitoring and Observability**  
Explored how AI can improve proactive performance monitoring, anomaly detection, and security visibility across emerging network environments.

**Infrastructure-as-Code for 5G Systems**  
Reviewed deployment and orchestration approaches for 5G RAN, core, and service-based interfaces using Infrastructure-as-Code methodologies.

**Secure SDN for Future Networks**  
Analyzed emerging cyber threats affecting software-defined 5G and future network architectures, with emphasis on mitigation and resilience.

**Critical Infrastructure Security Architecture**  
Investigated decentralized trust, federated identity, and zero trust enforcement models for cross-sector critical infrastructure protection.

**Post-Quantum Infrastructure Resilience**  
Studied migration strategies for high-value infrastructure systems that must remain secure against future quantum-capable adversaries.
